CUSTOMER SCENARIOS

Three contexts. Three hypotheses to verify.

Dental labs

Would a closer-to-finish adjustment option reduce the need to remake, reship or repeat conversations when shade needs attention?

Lab + clinic

Do teams value a clearer treatment path when a patient is still present? The answer depends on case type, compliance and collaboration.

Distributors

Would a demonstrable workflow help explain the outcome and training proposition better than selling components separately?

WORKFLOW

Where should the solution help in the real customer journey?

01 · IdentifyRecognize a shade or appearance gap during try-in, acceptance or discussion.
02 · DecideDetermine whether the case needs remaking, return shipment or a suitable adjustment path.
03 · PreparePrepare the materials and support tools under confirmed instructions.
04 · Glaze & fireOperate according to validated material, programme and training requirements.
05 · VerifyReview the result, document the issue and decide the next delivery step.

Evidence boundary: this page does not claim that equipment, materials or a procedure guarantees a shade result. Outcomes depend on materials, design, programme, calibration, firing conditions and operator experience.
